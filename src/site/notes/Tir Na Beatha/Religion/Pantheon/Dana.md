#### Myths, Legends, and Folklore

###### The First Exhale (Eladrin Myth)
> **Note: This Myth is told in the Eladrin Poetic Form of [[Tir Na Beatha/Cultural Matters/Sociology/Eladrin Poetic Forms#An Briste "The Fracture"\|An Briste]] and is performed by a poet who burns a massive, dried oak leaf from the Musewood. During the performance, the performers skin turns a sickly, rotting grey during the Shatter, before erupting into brilliant, vibrant color on the final line**
>  
>*(The Intake - The poet's voice is rigid, chanting in perfect, unyielding rhythm. They hold the unlit leaf in trembling hands.)*
>*The grey consumed the ancient stone,*
>*And chewed the marrow from the bone.*
>*We stood before the choking tide,*
>*With nowhere left to run or hide.*
>*The Mother opened up her chest,*
>*To draw the poison from the rest.*
>*She drank the venom, drank the blight,*
>*To pull us blindly through the night.*
>
>*(The Shatter - The poet lights the leaf. Their voice breaks completely. The structure vanishes into a panicked, screaming crescendo. They speak as fast as they can.)*
>*The rot is in her lungs! The sickness blackens the sun!*
>*Do not turn away! You must let it break you to survive it!*
>*She swallows the plague, it rips through the roots, it screams in the blood!*
>*Feel the decay! Bleed with it! If you build a wall the sorrow will only drown you!*
>*Let the despair crack your ribs open, let the terror shatter the glass!*
>*Hold the plague, hold the horror, let it burn until there is nothing left to—*
>
>*(The Exhale - Whispered in absolute, peaceful silence just as the flame bites their fingers and the ash falls.)*
>*...exhale, and step into the woods.*
