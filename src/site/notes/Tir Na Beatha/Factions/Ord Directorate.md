#### Summary
> The Ord Directorate is the ruling faction of the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarves]] in [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/Region/Ceardlachd Forest\|Ceardlachd Forest]]. Ruled by Ord-Ri [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Torin Iron-Heart\|Torin Iron-Heart]], the Directorate is pursuing Order and control over the chaotic nature of the [[Tir Na Beatha/01 -Tir Na Beatha\|Tir]] for the prosperity of the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarves]] and for all [[Tir Na Beatha/02 - Tuatha\|Tuatha]].  

#### Core Values
> The Ord Directorate believes that to resist and overcome [[Tir Na Beatha/Cultural Matters/Metaphysics/The Anord\|The Anord]] requires great discipline, strength, and order. A set and direct line of command from the top down brokers no arguments and invites no chaos. Deviating from the Will of the Ord-Ri is tantamount to insurrection and dealt with swiftly. Those who do not wish to contribute to the prosperity of their people are sent to attend the Ceard-Scoil's - "The School's of the Forge" where they may learn to see their true place among the bigger picture. 
> It is a common saying amongst the Ord Directorate of "Buail. Múnlaigh. Freastal.", which means "Strike. Shape. Serve." This saying speaks to the importance of the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarves]] taking a firm hand in directing the fate of all [[Tir Na Beatha/02 - Tuatha\|Tuatha]]. The Ord Directorate have three key founding tenets - The Wood Must Yield. The Nail Must Hold. The iron Must Flow.

###### The Wood Must Yield
> Nature is chaotic. Left on its own, it grows wild and tangled and consumes all in its path. It must be cut, planed, and worked into something useful. Give the Wild a purpose and it will flourish for the good of all. 

###### The Nail Must Hold
> The State is the Structure. The creation and maintenance of the Structure requires each component to be well-crafted and to full-fill it's purpose. Thus, an individual has no purpose other than to hold the Structure together. A loose  nail is to be hammered down, and a broken nail is to be pulled out. 

###### The Iron Must Flow
> Iron is the destiny of the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarves]]. Their connection to [[Tir Na Beatha/Religion/Pantheon/Govannon\|Govannon]] gives them the skill and the strength to shape it into great implements of change and prosperity. Clinging to the old ways will only invite the chaos of [[Tir Na Beatha/Cultural Matters/Metaphysics/The Anord\|The Anord]].

#### History
###### [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Torin Iron-Heart\|Torin]] Leads Clan [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/City/Ceardaigh\|Ceardaigh]]
> In the year 1540, [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Torin Iron-Heart\|Torin]] [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/City/Ceardaigh\|Ceardaigh]] is selected to lead his clan. At the age of 28, he is one of the youngest ever chosen to lead a Dwarven Clan and he approaches this task with passion and zealotry. He begins to lead the clan away from traditional logging and uses their influence to monopolize the burgeoning trade of peat iron. 

###### The Iron Scrip Coup
> Believing the [[Tir Na Beatha/Cultural Matters/Physical/Currency & Trade\|knots]] to be soft and weak and fundamentally unstable as a form of currency, and wanting to increase the influence of [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarves]] in the [[Tir Na Beatha/01 -Tir Na Beatha\|Tir]], [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Torin Iron-Heart\|Torin]] creates the Iron Scrip in 1551 and forces all merchants within [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/City/Ceardaigh\|Ceardaigh]] to conduct business only in Scrips. This catches the attention of the [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Weave-mothers\|Weave-mothers]] and in 1552, a joint operation of the [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Weave-mothers\|Weave-mothers]] and the [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Cailleotairi\|Cailleotairi]] apprehend [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Torin Iron-Heart\|Torin]] and sentence  him to imprisonment in [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/Landmark/Dún Réamheola\|Dún Réamheola]] for 5 years.
> 

###### [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Torin Iron-Heart\|Torin]] Appointed as Delegate to [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Council of Aite-Briethe\|Council of Aite-Briethe]]
> After his successor of Clan [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/City/Ceardaigh\|Ceardaigh]] dies from natural causes, [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Torin Iron-Heart\|Torin]] is once again selected to lead the Clan in the year 1558. In 1560, he appoints himself as the Dwarven Delegate to serve on the [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Council of Aite-Briethe\|Council of Aite-Briethe]]. There he uses his voice and vote to argue for the abolition of the [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Weave-mothers\|Weave-mothers]] and for the [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Cailleotairi\|Cailleotairi]] to be placed firmly under control of the [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Council of Aite-Briethe\|Council]]. He is largely unsuccessful, however, this gains him more support amongst the Dwarven clans.

###### [[Tir Na Beatha/History/War of the Severed Root\|War of the Severed Root]]
>  Dwarven logging enterprises in [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/Region/Fossmore Forest\|Fossmore Forest]] are attacked by Wood Elven hit-squads. [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Torin Iron-Heart\|Torin]] returns to [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/Region/Ceardlachd Forest\|Ceardlachd Forest]] to take command of the Dwarven forces and leads them in strikes against [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Wood Elves\|Wood Elf]] settlements on the borders. This escalates into a full-blown war between the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarves]] and the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Wood Elves\|Wood Elves]] beginning in the year 1568.

###### [[Tir Na Beatha/History/Treaty of Green Ash\|Treaty of Green Ash]]
> In 1571, the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarves]] are defeated after [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Wood Elves\|Wood Elves]] capture [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/City/Tre Coedwig\|Tre Coedwig]], a prominent center of the logging industry and trading hub. The Treaty sees key territories ceded to the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Wood Elves\|Wood Elves]], a strict embargo on any logging in [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/Region/Fossmore Forest\|Fossmore Forest]], and the removal of key iron trade routes from [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/Region/Draigsreach Peninsula\|Draigsreach Peninsula]] through [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Wood Elves\|Wood Elf]] territory. [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Torin Iron-Heart\|Torin]] refuses to sign this treaty, but is over-ridden by the traditionalist Old Guard leading rival major clans.

###### The Years of Rust
> Following the defeat and the signing of the Treaty, the Dwarven clans are hit hard by economic troubles. [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Torin Iron-Heart\|Torin]] delivers fiery speeches to masses of [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarves]] gathered in that taverns and drinking halls, declaring that the [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Traditionalists\|Traditionalists]] have failed the Dwarven people. He gathers more support amongst veterans and the lower clans, and uses the belief that Weave-Mother [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Weave-mothers\|Silk-Matron]]'s have been unfairly ruling against Dwarven merchants in trade disputes to stir up more animosity for non-[[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarves]]. 

###### The [[Tir Na Beatha/History/Rogue Scholar's Crisis\|Rogue Scholar's Crisis]]
> Dwarven traditionalist forces are called to aid the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Gnomes\|Gnomes]] during the [[Tir Na Beatha/History/Rogue Scholar's Crisis\|Rogue Scholar's Crisis]] of 1575. [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Torin Iron-Heart\|Torin]] see's this as the opportunity he needed and puts out a call to all loyal and patriotic [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarves]] to join him in marching on the clans to restore the Honour of the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarves]]. His gathered forces overwhelm what meager resistance there is, and Clan leaders are executed during the [[Tir Na Beatha/History/Night of Shattered Shields\|Night of Shattered Shields]]. 

###### Ord Directorate Consolidated
> [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Torin Iron-Heart\|Torin]] oversees the election of new clan leaders who are loyal to him. With loyalists now leading all of the major clans, and popular support amongst the lesser clans, he declares that the [[Tir Na Beatha/History/Rogue Scholar's Crisis\|Rogue Scholar's Crisis]] was a result of weak leadership and that it would take strength and discipline to ensure that such a fate can never befall the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarves]]. He declares himself the Ord-Ri of the new Ord Directorate.
