#### Summary
> The Dwarven Traditionalists are a decentralized and underground movement dedicated to preserving the "Old Ways" of their people. This philosophy is built on the belief that true order is something to be carefully crafted, that order can not be imposed. Prior to the rise of the [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Ord Directorate\|Ord Directorate]], they led the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarves]] in a democracy guided by the elected High Bench, where the Gaisce, Major and Faoi clans were considered equal pillars of a shared community. They value consensus, deep rooted honour, and the deliberate and slow pursuit of mastery of their craft. They believe in a strict symbiosis with nature. To a Traditionalist, a tree planter is just as important as the lumberjack. 
> 
> Stripped of their leadership during the massacre of the [[Tir Na Beatha/History/Night of Shattered Shields\|Night of Shattered Shields]], they now operate without a formal hierarchy. They remain bound together through the passage of ancestral heirlooms and the mantra "Deep roots hold firm". 

#### Core Values
> Traditionalists follow the Old Ways of the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarves]]. They stand in contrast to the [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Ord Directorate\|Ord Directorate]], which has changed Dwarven society into one where authority is a ladder and mastery over others is a virtue. They fear that the mass extraction of resources upsets the balance and strengthens [[Tir Na Beatha/Cultural Matters/Metaphysics/The Anord\|The Anord]], and that mass production devalues Dwarven crafting traditions developed over centuries. 

###### Order is Crafted
> Dwarven Traditionalists believe that peace, prosperity, and stability are not achieved in a day. They approach these goals much like they do their master works. A Dwarven artisan would rather spend a year crafting the perfect piece of furniture or tool than rush it and create something that breaks in a day. To a Traditionalist, the brutal control exerted by [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Torin Iron-Heart\|Torin]] and his Directorate is brittle and would shatter under the slightest amount of pressure. 

###### Deep Roots Hold Firm
> Traditionalists hold to deep bonds between Clans and others. To them, these bonds strengthen the unity of their people and create resilient communities that can hold against what ever challenges the [[Tir Na Beatha/01 -Tir Na Beatha\|Tir]] may present to them. A [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarf]] should always seek to help another [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarf]] or their allies. 

###### Harmony is Balance
> The Old Ways call for balance in all things. This is evident in traditional Dwarven justice, and in the way that they do honor to nature and the land they call home. There can and should be no extraction of resources without finding a suitable way to pay back or replace what is taken. 

###### Consensus is King
> Dwarven Traditionalists hold to the old ways of making decisions, in which understanding is developed through conversation and respectful debate. This fosters trust and respect between parties, and leads to a deepening of the bonds between them. 

#### Hierarchy
> The Traditionalists lack a formal hierarchical structure or organization. To be a Traditionalist requires only that a [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarf]] honours the Old Ways and to resist the [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Ord Directorate\|Ord Directorate]] where possible. Groups of like-minded [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarf]]'s may exist, but it is rare to find any that would identify themselves as a Traditionalist openly. 

#### History
###### [[Tir Na Beatha/History/War of Summer's Favour\|War of Summer's Favour]]
> In the early days of their history, the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarves]] sought domination over the land. They believed that in gaining the upper hand against the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Feyfolk\|Fey]], and other Tuatha, they could eliminate the threat posed by [[Tir Na Beatha/Cultural Matters/Metaphysics/The Anord\|The Anord]] and impose a lasting peace. Their defeat during the [[Tir Na Beatha/History/War of Summer's Favour\|War of Summer's Favour]] showed them that others would always resist, and that order through force was only an illusion and could break in a moment. As Dwarven carpentry techniques advanced in complexity, they realized that true mastery came from learning about and working with each individual piece of wood and began to apply this concept to diplomacy. 



###### [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Historical/Lord Tarsus\|The Thread-Breaker]]'s War
> During the chaotic years of the Thread-Breakers War, the Traditionalists found their ideals of balance severely tested. Dwarven lands were besieged on two fronts by [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Wood Elves\|Wood Elf]] warbands and the very [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Tieflings\|Tiefling]] exiles the Traditionalists had secretly aided in the past. Blindsided by the disastrous Rune-Breach Folly caused by their Gnomish allies in the [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/City/Cogswyr\|Cogswyr]] Syndicate, the faction turned away from further martial escalation and leaned heavily into their diplomatic roots. Relying on the [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Cailleotairi\|Cailleotairi]] for reinforcement, the Traditionalist leaders used their influence within the Dwarven delegation to champion these neutral warriors as the official peacekeepers of the realm. True to their belief in consensus and harmony, it was the Traditionalists who successfully argued for the foundational tenet of the [[Tir Na Beatha/History/The Weave-Mother's Peace\|The Weave-Mother's Peace]]: that despite their differences, all Tuatha share a common heritage and must prioritize the stability of the [[Tir Na Beatha/01 -Tir Na Beatha\|Tir]] above the ambitions of any single tribe.

###### [[Tir Na Beatha/History/Discovery of Iron\|Discovery of Iron]]
> When the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Lizard-folk\|Lizard-folk]] discovered iron in the [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/Region/Draigsreach Peninsula\|Draigsreach Peninsula]], the Dwarven Traditionalists were deeply cautious. While other craftsmen rushed to set up trade routes and forge these new, [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Feyfolk\|fey]]-harming weapons, the Traditionalists hesitated. They understood the value of iron, but believed that taking such a powerful gift from the earth required finding a way to repay the land and maintain balance. Unfortunately, their warnings were ignored by the rest of their people. As the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Wood Elves\|Wood Elves]] began blockading the iron trade out of anger over this corruption of nature, the Traditionalists could only watch as the rising tensions paved the way for the [[Tir Na Beatha/History/War of the Severed Root\|War of the Severed Root]].

###### [[Tir Na Beatha/History/War of the Severed Root\|War of the Severed Root]] and the [[Tir Na Beatha/History/Treaty of Green Ash\|Treaty of Green Ash]]
> During the [[Tir Na Beatha/History/War of the Severed Root\|War of the Severed Root]], the Traditionalists watched their worst fears materialize. Their earlier warnings about the unchecked expansion of the iron and logging trades were completely ignored, leading directly to a brutal guerrilla war with the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Wood Elves\|Wood Elves]] over the desecration of [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/Region/Fossmore Forest\|Fossmore Forest]]. When the war turned against the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarves]] and the vital hub of [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/City/Tre Coedwig\|Tre Coedwig]] was captured, the Traditionalist leaders of the Major Clans saw a necessary opportunity to end the bloodshed. Prioritizing ecological balance over industrial greed, they overrode the objections of military leaders like [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Torin Iron-Heart\|Torin Iron-Heart]] and quickly signed the [[Tir Na Beatha/History/Treaty of Green Ash\|Treaty of Green Ash]]. They accepted the strict logging embargoes and territorial losses in a desperate bid to restore harmony to the land. However, this pragmatic surrender cost them dearly; their willingness to yield gave [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Torin Iron-Heart\|Torin]] the exact political ammunition he needed to brand the Old Guard as weak, setting the stage for his fiery populist uprising.

###### The [[Tir Na Beatha/History/Night of Shattered Shields\|Night of Shattered Shields]]
> During the [[Tir Na Beatha/History/Rogue Scholar's Crisis\|Rogue Scholar's Crisis]] of 1575, the Traditionalist leaders sent their forces to aid their [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Gnomes\|Gnomish]] allies. [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Torin Iron-Heart\|Torin Iron-Heart]] used their absence to strike. He marched his loyalist forces into [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/City/Ceardaigh\|Ceardaigh]] and executed the remaining Traditionalist leaders of the Major Clans—specifically those who had not already been forced out through his rigged elections. This massacre became known as the [[Tir Na Beatha/History/Night of Shattered Shields\|Night of Shattered Shields]]. With their leadership wiped out and [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Torin Iron-Heart\|Torin]]'s loyalists installed in their place to form the [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Ord Directorate\|Ord Directorate]], the Traditionalists were stripped of their political power. Today, they are forced to operate entirely in the shadows as an underground resistance.
