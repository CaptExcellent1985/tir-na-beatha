#### Summary
> Tall and beautiful, with generally slender builds, the Eladrin of the Musewood are descended from the first proto-Tuatha to have fled from Aite-Briethe during the Sundering Plague. Their ancestors did not fear the chaos of the Anord, instead viewing its chaotic nature as an affirmation that life is fleeting and that it must be lived fully. Where some may view the Eladrin as being slaves to their passions, the Eladrin see themselves as being freed by them. 
> 
> Seeing themselves as the children of Dana and Taranis, an Eladrins personality is ever-changing and as unpredictable as the storms of their Father. These varying personality types fall under several archetypes, named for one of the four seasons of the Tir. An Eladrin who is enthusiastically optimistic, and filled with the creative spirit is said to be of the Spring Aspect. Fiery in speech, equal parts protective and confrontational, a Summer Eladrin lives fully in the sway of their emotions whether they be anger or love. Autumn Aspect Eladrin are pragmatic, but generous - they care for the needs of the community while understanding that all good things must come to an end. Stoic and sorrowful, a Winter Aspect Eladrin is driven by an analytical mind and seeks to conserve their passions in silence. 
> 
> Driven by their passions as they are, Eladrin are drawn to the arts. Their love of music, theatre, poetry, and sculpture brings them to the other tribes to share and exchange culture freely. The many wineries present in the Musewood are known for producing exhilarating and intoxicating vintages, that are infused with the emotional energy of their vintners. No matter what thing an Eladrin is called to do, it is known by all Tuatha that they will commit themselves wholly to it - so long as the season holds. 

#### Culture
> For the Eladrin, Discord and Chaos are natural things. Life is change, and the world is unpredictable. Their art is a reflection of this. Eladrin fashion and paintings are clashes between hyper-saturated colours that seem almost to vibrate. Their music can be disorienting, with sudden changes in pacing and rhythm, and dissonant chords peppered throughout. Eladrin myths and legends center around their role in the Tir as conduits. Their central legend, in which Dana leads them safely through a sea of decay and despair by absorbing it and releasing it behind them, is a reflection of their commitment to feel every emotion good or bad. The Eladrin believe that the chaotic and emotional turmoil of the world flows through them to be dissipated peacefully. They express themselves freely, and [[Tir Na Beatha/Cultural Matters/Sociology/Eladrin Poetic Forms\|Eladrin Poetic Forms]] reflects this.

###### Social Structure
> The foundational Eladrin social unit is the "Copse". The Copse is a temporary family unit, in which several adults and children resonate with the same seasonal energy or passions.  Because of this, emotional kinship is valued over blood relations, and when an adults season shifts they leave the Copse to find another more in line with their current mood. Child-rearing begins communally, with Spring Eladrin of The First Bloom nurturing children in sunlit nurseries of living wood. When a Child experiences their first "Shatter" - emotional outburst - they are considered to be a teenager and then join their first Copse. Here they learn from the adults how to channel and safely purge their emotional energy. By the time an Eladrin reaches adulthood, they will have been raised by a number of different parents across the four seasons. 
> 
> Eladrin communities are overseen by the Court of Seasons. Leadership is fluid and everchanging, a meritocracy in which the brightest burning or most cold and calculating Eladrin becomes the Crown Muse and abdicates peacefully when their season inevitably shifts. These Crown Muses direct similarly aspected Eladrin called to public service, with each Season devoted to particular roles:> 
> - **Spring** Eladrin find themselves in either the Shaping Chorus and guiding the growth of new buildings, bridges, and archways, or within The First Bloom where they oversee child-rearing, education, and the planting of crops. They may also be drawn to serve as Vernal Weavers; the match-makers of Eladrin society. 
> - **Summer** Eladrin can serve as members of the Grand Crescendo, organizing and funding arts festivals, musical performances, and the installation of public art. They may also be drawn to the Crimson Tempest, meting out justice, settling public debates (which are conducted as loud screaming matches) and defending the borders of the Musewood. 
> - **Autumn** Eladrin, being generous yet pragmatic, are often in charge of managing the resources of the community. Those who serve in the Gathering of the Vines are responsible for harvests across the Musewood, while those Autumn Eladrin who find themselves in the Winds Exchange oversee the flow of trade between Eladrin settlements to ensure that all Eladrin are well taken care of. 
> - **Winter** Eladrin can be ruthlessly analytical and most concerned with strict conservation. Serving in The Deep Root, they can be archivists or conservation authorities, where they determine when the ritualistic Kindling of the Hearth is appropriate. Within the Weight of the Frost, Winter Eladrin are called upon to sentence those found guilty by Eladrin serving in the Crimson Tempest.
