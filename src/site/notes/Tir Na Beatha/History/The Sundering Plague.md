#### Quick Summary

> A great illness began to afflict Proto-Tuatha. Within a matter of time, it came to be believed that the area itself was cursed and the Proto-Tuatha struck out into the [[Tir Na Beatha/01 -Tir Na Beatha\|Tir]]. Those who headed north and followed the coast of [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/Region/Cuan Realta\|Cuan Realta]] settled in the area of the [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/Region/Musewood\|Musewood]] and would become the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Eladrin\|Eladrin]]. Some headed south of [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/City/Aite-Briethe\|Aite-Briethe]] and convinced that the disease was caused by the technology of the [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Ancient Ones\|Ancient Ones]] became the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Wood Elves\|Wood Elves]]. Some opted to stay behind to tend to those who were too sick to travel. These eventually became the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Twilight Elves\|Twilight Elves]], who were convinced by a [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Feyfolk\|Fey]]-Spirit to head to the North-west, to the dead-lands known as [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/Region/Na Tirean Marbh\|Na Tirean Marbh]] and to usher the dead to the after-life. This also would become the March of the Dead.
