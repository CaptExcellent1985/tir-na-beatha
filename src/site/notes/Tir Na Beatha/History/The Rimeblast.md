#### Quick Summary

> The [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Tieflings\|Tieflings]] unleash potent wild magic called the Rimeblast that destroys the original foundation of [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/City/Ceardaigh\|Ceardaigh]]. The [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarves]] retreat and resolve that order must be crafted, not imposed. The horror of the Rimeblast inspires the early [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Gnomes\|Gnomes]] to begin dedicated study of magic in the [[Tir Na Beatha/01 -Tir Na Beatha\|Tir]].
