## Session Summary
Aoife revealed that a wave of suicidal compulsion in the **Market District** was caused by a **tainted knot** known as the **Dark Knot**, which was traded to a stall. She explained that these [[Tir Na Beatha/Cultural Matters/Physical/Currency & Trade\|knots]] are made from **[[Tir Na Beatha/Cultural Matters/Physical/Lumina Silk\|lumina silk]]** by the **Weave Mothers** and can be corrupted by flawed making or intense emotions. The party then met **[[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Tekia The Silent\|Tekia the Silent]]**, a [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Goliath\|goliath]] from the **[[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Guild of the Gathering Storm\|Guild of the Gathering Storm]]**, at **The Rain Barrel**. Tekia denied the guild's involvement in counterfeiting and blamed a splinter group called the **Crimson Daggers** operating on the riverbank, offering a bounty on them.

The group sought out [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Kobolds\|kobolds]] in the sewers, heading toward the **River District** and crossing into **Artisan’s Haven**. After locating a [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Kobolds\|kobold]] sorcerer’s shop and the **Scale Pile**, they met a [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Kobolds\|kobold]] who led them to **Nox**, a maintenance [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Kobolds\|kobold]]. Nox displayed unusual apathy, refusing payment for information about the sewer layout. After some intimidation from **[[Tir Na Beatha/The Campaign/PCs/Sorn Talabrina\|Sorn]]**, Nox described the sewer layout and warned of big insects and voices in the sewers, directing the party to an outspout near the docks.

The party then sought the help of the **Dagda Grove druids** to locate a hidden passage between the grove and the **River District**. They encountered **Elowen**, who used druidic magic to learn about **Tuatha** sightings near the riverbank. Afterward, the group found a [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Kobolds\|kobold]]-sized tunnel entrance and advanced to the main sewer access, where they spotted a sewer entrance guarded by brutes from the **Crimson Daggers**. A skirmish ensued, with **[[Tir Na Beatha/The Campaign/PCs/Grix Gildred\|Grix]]**, **[[Tir Na Beatha/The Campaign/PCs/Kallista Shadowhorn\|Kallista]]**, **[[Tir Na Beatha/The Campaign/PCs/Sorn Talabrina\|Sorn]]**, and others engaging in combat against the guards, resulting in the archer being killed and the party continuing their assault on the remaining foes and the emerging **Crimson Dagger** members.

The session concluded with the party in the midst of battle, having successfully eliminated one archer and continuing to confront the remaining threats from the **Crimson Daggers** at the sewer entrance, setting the stage for further conflict and exploration of the sewers and their mysteries.



## Key Moments
### Etha's Revelation of the Dark Knot
In the Market District, Aoife reveals a tainted artifact called a "Dark Knot," made from [[Tir Na Beatha/Cultural Matters/Physical/Lumina Silk\|lumina silk]] by the Weave Mothers. These [[Tir Na Beatha/Cultural Matters/Physical/Currency & Trade\|knots]] absorb emotional energy and spread despair. The group must choose to confront the source of corruption or seek the Weave Mothers for answers, feeling the urgency and responsibility to act before more victims are claimed.

### Bounty on [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/The Crimson Daggers\|the Crimson Daggers]]
Inside The Rain Barrel, hushed conversations mix with the scent of damp wood and ale. [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Tekia The Silent\|Tekia the Silent]] denies her guild's involvement in the counterfeiting scandal, blaming [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/The Crimson Daggers\|the Crimson Daggers]]. With a bounty on them, she urges the party to act. Kroc-gar suggests tailing a gang member, while [[Tir Na Beatha/The Campaign/PCs/Grix Gildred\|Grix]] warns of their violence. The party strategizes, knowing their decision carries significant consequences.

### Confrontation in the Dagda Grove
The party enters Dagda Grove, filled with floral scents. A frustrated Summer-aspect [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Eladrin\|eladrin]] confronts them, but another [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Eladrin\|eladrin]] intervenes, calming the situation and attracting nearby druids. Kroc-gar suggests engaging the druids for insights on local trees and Tuatha activity, while [[Tir Na Beatha/The Campaign/PCs/Jexim Hidker\|Jexim]] hesitates. The [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Eladrin\|eladrin]]’s intervention hints at potential alliances within the grove's wisdom.
