#### Summary
> Comprising several caverns in the upper level of [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/City/Cri Chnuas\|Cri Chnuas]], the Clutch of the Arcane is home to the famed academy of [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Kobolds\|Kobold]] Sorcerer's. It can be accessed by heading North from the [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/Locale/Scaled Market\|Scaled Market]] and is in close proximity to the [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/Locale/Ember-Belly Inn\|Ember-Belly Inn]], Cinder-cask Brewing, and [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/Locale/The Cracked Shell\|The Cracked Shell]]. It's library, though not as large or diverse as the one found in [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/City/Eolas\|Eolas]], serves [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Kobolds\|Kobold]]-kind well. 

#### Notable Features

######  Hall of the Commons
> The almost-imperceptible buzzing of magic and [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Feyfolk\|fey]]-energy crackles so strongly in this cavern that even those not sensitive to it can feel its presence. Bluetile stone glimmers beneath levitating motes of wizard-light and the cavern is warmed by a constantly burning magical fire tended to by arcane neophytes. Stone benches line the walls where those seeking the counsel of Clutch magicians or to gain admittance to study here wait patiently to be heard. A crooked and wizened [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Kobolds\|Kobold]] by the name of [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Firebomb\|Firebomb]] manages the front-desk, typically thumbing absent-mindedly through a weathered book.

###### The Acolytes Kiln
> Fine [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarves]] crafted wooden desks face a single lectern at the rear of this cavern that serves as the primary lecture hall for the [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/Locale/Clutch of the Arcane\|Clutch of the Arcane]]. Students attend classes and guest lectures here, and there is ample room to the rear and sides of the cavern for them to practice their casting, enchanting, and alchemy.
