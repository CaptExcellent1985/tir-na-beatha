#### Physical Appearance

> Barrel-chested and imposing, Torins age only adds to the strength and force of will he exudes. With silver hair and braided beard, he views the world with gleaming blue eyes that command the attention of those who stand in his presence. His suit of plate armor, forged from black iron and periodically venting steam, is as unyielding as Torin himself. 

#### Personality

###### The Hammer of [[Tir Na Beatha/Religion/Pantheon/Govannon\|Govannon]]
> Torin see's himself as the living embodiment of [[Tir Na Beatha/Religion/Pantheon/Govannon\|Govannon]]'s hammer, as the implement that the God uses to  forge and shape the world into order. His word is absolute, each sentence a command to be carried out with unflinching resolve. He asks no questions, relying on skilled interrogators to speak for him when the need arises. 

###### Iron Over Wood
> Torin views the [[Tir Na Beatha/History/Discovery of Iron\|Discovery of Iron]] as a gift to the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarves]] and as the fulfillment of a prophecy. He respects the old ways of the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarves]], paying lip service to the hard-working carpenters and woodsmen of the Tribe, but he has become obsessed with Iron. To Torin, it is cold, rigid, and unyielding - qualities he wishes to instill in his people. 

#### Biography 

> Torin's long life has been spent in the pursuit of power. From a young age, he saw the [[Tir Na Beatha/History/Discovery of Iron\|Discovery of Iron]] as the fulfillment of a divine prophecy, that the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarves]] would come to lead the [[Tir Na Beatha/02 - Tuatha\|Tuatha]] into mastery over the [[Tir Na Beatha/Cultural Matters/Metaphysics/The Anord\|Anord]] and the [[Tir Na Beatha/01 -Tir Na Beatha\|Tir]]. Becoming the leader of Clan [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/City/Ceardaigh\|Ceardaigh]] in his mid-20's, he was imprisoned in [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/Landmark/Dún Réamheola\|Dún Réamheola]] for a short time after he attempted to force the clan's to abandon the knot currency in favor of his own - the Iron Scrip. Shortly after gaining his freedom, he began to consolidate his power and influence with the other clan's. He served as the Dwarven delegate on the [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Council of Aite-Briethe\|Council of Aite-Briethe]], where he argued for the dissolution of the Weave-mother's and for bringing the [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Cailleotairi\|Cailleotairi]] under the direct control of the Council. He returned to [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/Region/Ceardlachd Forest\|Ceardlachd Forest]] when the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Dwarves\|Dwarves]] warred with the [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Wood Elves\|Wood Elves]] and used the opportunity to declare himself as the Ord-Ri of the [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Ord Directorate\|Ord Directorate]], and gave himself the surname of Iron-Heart.
