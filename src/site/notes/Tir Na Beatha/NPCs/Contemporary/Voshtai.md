#### Physical Appearance

> Of average height and medium build, Voshtai's physical appearance would be un-imposing if not for his strong character and storied history with the [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Cailleotairi\|Cailleotairi]]. Possessed of green scales and a dappled yellow tail-fin that can sometimes give away the emotions he tries to hide. 

#### Personality

###### Sanctity of the Oath
> Above all else, Voshtai views his duty to the [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Cailleotairi\|Cailleotairi]] and to the [[Tir Na Beatha/02 - Tuatha\|Tuatha]] to be paramount. Honour means everything to Voshtai, as well as the keeping of one's word. The [[Tir Na Beatha/Cultural Matters/Sociology/The Verdant Oath\|Verdant Oath]] is more sacred to Voshtai than any decree of the God's. 

 
###### The Bones of His Mentors
> His strongest bond is to his deceased mentor, [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Vethos\|Vethos]]. His spear, fashioned from [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Vethos\|Vethos]]'s own bones, is a physical tether to the man who saw potential in a "runt." He is also inextricably bound to [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Elara of the Whispering Moss\|Elara]] (his only remaining anchor to his youth) and the memory of Saoirse, whose literal body now serves as the shield he is sworn to protect.

###### Burden of the Bridge
>Voshtai suffers from survivor’s guilt transformed into hyper-rigid perfectionism. He blames the "lack of discipline" for [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Cormac McCarthy\|Cormac]]’s death rather than the chaos of wild magic. This makes him a demanding, sometimes suffocating leader who struggles to trust "luck" or "abandon," fearing that any moment of joy or recklessness will lead to another [[Tir Na Beatha/History/Rogue Scholar's Crisis\|Fomorian Breach]].

###### Redemption through Instruction
>It is through his careful instruction and demonstration that Voshtai believes he can make up for his failing's during the [[Tir Na Beatha/History/Rogue Scholar's Crisis\|Fomorian Breach]] and the [[Tir Na Beatha/History/Rogue Scholar's Crisis\|Rogue Scholar's Crisis]].  By guiding new [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Cailleotairi\|Foghlaimi]] and instilling in them the importance of precision and planning, then no other [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Cailleotairi\|Laochra]] will need to suffer the same fate of his. 

#### Biography 

> Serving as [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Cailleotairi\|Saois]] to all new [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Cailleotairi\|Foghlaimi]] who survive [[Tir Na Beatha/Cultural Matters/Sociology/The Proving\|The Proving]] and join the ranks of the [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Cailleotairi\|Cailleotairi]], Voshtai is a stoic [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Lizard-folk\|Lizard-folk]] warrior whose precision in battle has served him well. 

###### Early Life
> Born to a Peat-Iron prospector, Voshtai was one of a clutch of 7 in [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/City/Bone Cairn\|Bone Cairn]]. He was the runt of the litter, and constantly bullied by his larger brothers and sisters. An elderly [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Lizard-folk\|Lizard-folk]] by the name of [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Vethos\|Vethos]] took pity on this runt and began to teach him principles of defence. [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Vethos\|Vethos]] also instilled in Voshtai the virtues of honour and of defending the weak. 
> 
> At the age of 11, [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Vethos\|Vethos]] was invited to participate in [[Tir Na Beatha/Cultural Matters/Sociology/The Proving\|The Proving]] after he foiled an attempt by [[Kozok the Broken Gaze\|Kozok the Broken Gaze]] to escape her imprisonment through the use of boggles. It is here when Voshtai learned of the [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Cailleotairi\|Cailleotairi]], of the [[Tir Na Beatha/Cultural Matters/Sociology/The Verdant Oath\|Verdant Oath]], and he swore that he would follow in his mentor's footsteps.
> 
> When Voshtai turned 15, he began to accompany his father on expeditions into the swamps of the [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/Region/Draigsreach Peninsula\|Draigsreach Peninsula]]. His first weapon was a spear he crafted himself from shards of obsidian and pieces of driftwood. It has broken many times in his early adventures, and he dutifully repaired it each time. It now rests in his quarters at [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/Locale/Siorlann\|Siorlann]], a reminder of his beginnings.

###### Initiation into the [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Cailleotairi\|Cailleotairi]]
> When Voshtai was 17, he uncovered a Tiefling smuggling operation that used Bucca (small Fey spirits with obsidian skin and wings) to locate iron shipments. He devised a plan to fool the Bucca's "locate object" magic, and single-handedly fought the Tiefling encampment. This earned him some renown amongst his fellow [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Lizard-folk\|Lizard-folk]], and it was not long before he received an invitation to participate in [[Tir Na Beatha/Cultural Matters/Sociology/The Proving\|The Proving]]. > 

###### 'The Sinking Stone'
> At [[Tir Na Beatha/Cultural Matters/Sociology/The Proving\|The Proving]], Voshtai was to meet the members of what would become his [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Cailleotairi\|Laochra]]. Nicknamed 'The Sinking Stone', his [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Cailleotairi\|Laochra]] had a reputation for surviving almost impossible odds. It was said that you could cast them into the deepest of waters and they would get to the bottom of it. 
> 
> 'The Sinking Stone' was made up of [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Cormac McCarthy\|Cormac McCarthy]], [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Elara of the Whispering Moss\|Elara of the Whispering Moss]], [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Kaelen Quick-Step\|Kaelen Quick-Step]], and [[Tir Na Beatha/NPCs/Contemporary/Saiorse the Unblinking\|Saiorse the Unblinking]].  The [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Cailleotairi\|Cailleotairi]] still tell legends of their many exploits, from the [[Hunting of Rot Tooth\|Hunting of Rot Tooth]], to the [[Siege of Trust Knot\|Siege of Trust Knot]], and the [[Grey-Rot Harvest\|Grey-Rot Harvest]].
