#### Summary
> Produced by the [[Tir Na Beatha/Cultural Matters/Physical/Lumina Moth\|Lumina Moth]], Lumina Silk is used by the [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Weave-mothers\|Weave-mothers]] to create the [[Tir Na Beatha/Cultural Matters/Physical/Currency & Trade\|knots]] that serve as the primary currency in the [[Tir Na Beatha/Tir Na Beatha Leaflet\|Tir]]. It's unique properties lends itself well to this and it is a strictly regulated commodity. 

#### Detailed Summary
###### Unique Properties
> Fine and soft, this material fluoresces in reaction to emotional energy. This property has allowed the [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Weave-mothers\|Weave-mothers]] and the council of [[Tir Na Beatha/Geography/City/Aite-Briethe\|Aite-Briethe]] to create a common currency for trade that is nigh-unto impossible to counterfeit.
