#### Summary

> The Verdant Oath is sworn by all new [[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Cailleotairi\|Cailleotairi]] after they survive [[Tir Na Beatha/Cultural Matters/Sociology/The Proving\|The Proving]]. It encapsulates their core values and symbolizes their dedication to the [[Tir Na Beatha/Tir Na Beatha Leaflet\|Tir]], all[[Tir Na Beatha/02 - Tuatha\|Tuatha]], and to each other. 

#### Detailed Summary

###### The Oath
> *As shadows shift and paths unfold, we stand united, strong and bold.To Tir’s heart true, our spears defend, our eyes pursue.*
> 
>*Fey whispers guide every stride, in nature's dance, we shall confide.Comrades true, our loyalty sworn, Cailleotairi, from dusk to dawn.*
