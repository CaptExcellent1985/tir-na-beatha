#### Summary
> **Tir Na Beatha** is a realm defined by the eternal struggle to impose structure upon the primal, shifting chaos known as **[[Tir Na Beatha/Cultural Matters/Metaphysics/The Anord\|The Anord]]**. The diverse **[[Tir Na Beatha/02 - Tuatha\|Tuatha]]** tribes have carved out a civilization where survival depends on maintaining a fragile pact with the land's fickle [[Tir Na Beatha/The Tribes/Feyfolk\|Fey]] spirits and the stabilized seasons. Peace is rigidly enforced by the **[[Tir Na Beatha/Factions/Cailleotairi\|Cailleotairi]]**, a stoic order of elite, neutral arbiters founded by exiles who rejected tribal loyalty for the greater good. In this world, ancient wood-craft rivals stone, emotions can curdle gold, and the line between civilization and the encroaching wild is held by those who have sworn to sever their own roots to protect the realm.
